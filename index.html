What it does

Halfhour shows every Irish supplier's dynamic electricity price for every half hour of today and tomorrow, and tells you the cheapest time to run the dryer, the dishwasher or charge the car.

It is for households on a dynamic tariff, or thinking of moving to one. Each supplier's own app shows its own price alone. Halfhour works out all of them side by side, so you can see where your price is going and how the suppliers compare.

It covers Electric Ireland, Energia, Bord Gáis Energy, Yuno Energy and PrePayPower. It compares dynamic tariffs only. Standard and time-of-use plans are not included.

No account, no ads, no tracking.

How it works

The price you pay

The Commission for Regulation of Utilities, the CRU, sets one structure for every Irish dynamic tariff. Each half hour's unit price has three parts:

  • The wholesale price, the same for every supplier, from the day-ahead market. The CRU caps this part at 50c per kWh.
  • Your supplier's base rate for the band the half hour falls in, night, day or the 17:00 to 19:00 peak. Some suppliers charge one rate all day.
  • VAT at 9%.
price = (wholesale, capped at 50c + base rate) × 1.09

A daily standing charge comes on top, whatever you use. The wholesale part has no floor, so a price below zero takes money off your bill.

Where the wholesale price comes from

Each morning the power exchange for the island of Ireland, SEMOpx, holds an auction for every half hour of the next day. The results are published around midday, which is when tomorrow fills in. Halfhour reads them from SEMOpx's public reports on your phone.

The market's day runs from 23:00 to 23:00 Irish time, so the first rows of tomorrow are tonight, from 23:00.

Why prices swing

Wind costs almost nothing to run once it is built, while gas plants pay for fuel and carbon. When it is windy and demand is low, prices fall towards zero. When the wind drops, or demand peaks in the evening, gas sets the price and it climbs. Halfhour shows EirGrid's wind figures beside the prices so you can see the two together.

Supplier rates

Each supplier's base rates and standing charges come from its own published price list. They are kept in a rates file published on this site, described below, so a supplier's change reaches the app without an app update.

How to use it

Setup

On first launch, accept the disclaimer and either tap Use Averages and Start, or tell Halfhour about your home: urban or rural, your yearly usage or your ESB Networks smart meter file, your supplier and the appliances you want to plan. Everything can be changed later in Settings.

Today

  • Your supplier's price right now, with a plain verdict, cheap, normal or dear, and when the next cheap stretch begins.
  • A card for each appliance with its best start and how long it runs, such as "Start 02:30 tomorrow, for 2h 43m, cost about €3.82". It says "Start now" once the best time has come.
  • Tap a card to set the appliance's figures and limits: Ready By, Earliest Start and Allowed Hours. Remind Me sets a notification for the best start.
  • Add an appliance with the + button: car, tumble dryer, washing machine, dishwasher, immersion, or your own.

Half Hours

  • A colour strip of every half hour, one column per supplier, green for cheap and red for dear, with the price in every cell. The current half hour is outlined and past ones are dimmed.
  • Tap a cell to see the wholesale price, the supplier's rate and the VAT behind it.
  • Switch between Today, Tomorrow and Analysis. Analysis explains the day: where your price goes, how the wholesale price moves and what the wind is doing, then how prices work.

Charts

Every supplier on one chart for today, tomorrow, seven days or thirty, or the same figures as a table. Tap a supplier's chip to hide or show its line. Hold and drag across the chart to read each line at that half hour.

Compare

What a year would cost on each dynamic tariff for your usage. Shifted moves your appliances to their cheapest times so you can see the difference. Pull down to check for new prices and rates.

Settings

  • Choose your supplier, which suppliers are shown and their order.
  • Turn on Spike Alert to be warned half an hour before a half hour above your chosen price.
  • In Supplier Rates, open a supplier to see the rates in use and a link to its price list. You can check a rate against your supplier's own app: enter the price it shows for the current half hour and Halfhour works out the base rate, and keeps your figure if it differs.

Widget

Add the Best Time Today widget to your Home Screen or Lock Screen, then edit it to choose an appliance. It shows that appliance's cheapest start today and how long it runs.

The rates file

The supplier rates Halfhour uses are published as a single JSON file:

https://fractaldoctor.github.io/Halfhour/tariffs.json

The figures are taken from each supplier's own published price list, linked from the file, and updated when a supplier changes its prices. Halfhour checks for a newer file every few hours. It uses a file only if it reads correctly and its figures are plausible, and falls back to the rates built into the app if this site cannot be reached.

What it holds

FieldMeaning
vatRateVAT on electricity, 0.09 for 9%
capCentsThe CRU's cap on the wholesale part, in cents per kWh
readOnThe date the file was last updated
suppliersOne entry per supplier
bandsEach band's name, start and end in minutes after midnight, and base rate in cents per kWh, excluding VAT
standingUrban, standingRuralStanding charges in euro a year, excluding VAT
extraChargesOther yearly charges, such as the PSO levy, excluding VAT
sourceURLThe supplier's own price list
readOn on a supplierThe date that supplier's figures were read

Rates in the file exclude VAT. Always check the price in your supplier's own app before you rely on it.

Support

Halfhour is developed and maintained by a single developer. If you need help or want to suggest an improvement:

  • Ask a question, about the app or the prices
  • Report a bug, something not working as expected
  • Report a rate, a supplier figure that looks out of date
  • Request a feature

Please note

Halfhour estimates prices and costs to help you plan. It is not your bill and it is not your supplier. Prices are worked out from the published day-ahead market price, the regulator's cap and each supplier's published rates as last read, and can be wrong or out of date. Always check the price in your supplier's app before you charge a car, run an appliance or switch supplier.
