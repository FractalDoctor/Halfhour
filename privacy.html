Halfhour Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Data collection

Halfhour does not collect, store or send any personal data. It needs no account, login or registration, and it never asks for your name, email, MPRN or location.

Network requests

Halfhour makes HTTPS requests to these public sources and no others:

  • reports.semopx.com, the day-ahead electricity prices published by SEMOpx
  • www.vargen.smartgriddashboard.com, EirGrid's wind output and forecast
  • fractaldoctor.github.io, this site, for the supplier rates file

The widget makes the same SEMOpx request when the app has not already fetched the day's prices. These requests carry no information about you, and no keys or credentials. A link you tap yourself, such as a supplier's price list or the App Store, opens in Safari or the App Store.

Data kept on your device

  • Cached prices for the last 30 days, the supplier rates in use, and any rates you confirm yourself.
  • Your settings and appliances, shared with the Halfhour widget through the app's own App Group on the same device.
  • If you import an ESB Networks smart meter file, a summary of your average day. The file itself and the summary never leave your phone.

Nothing is stored in iCloud or shared with other apps. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Notifications

Reminders and spike alerts are local notifications scheduled on your phone. No notification server is involved.

Tips

Tips are optional in-app purchases handled entirely by Apple. Halfhour never sees your payment details and learns only that a purchase completed.

Analytics and tracking

Halfhour contains no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking code, and no third-party code that collects data.

Children

Halfhour collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address. The app will not change how it handles data without a matching update here.

Contact

For questions about this policy or the app, open an issue on GitHub.
